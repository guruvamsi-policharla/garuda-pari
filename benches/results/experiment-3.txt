║  3b. ZK-Pari payment circuits — BLS12-381                            ║
║      private transfer: R_send, R_recv, and operation-hiding R_op     ║
║      position nullifiers + sparse Merkle tree; Pedersen vs Poseidon  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Threads:  keygen/verify single-threaded; prove measured on 1, 8, and all 18 cores.
Hashes:   one collision-resistant hash for Merkle nodes and commitments,
          instantiated as Pedersen/Jubjub (8-bit byte windows) or Poseidon
          (width 3, alpha 5, 8 + 57 rounds). No PRF anywhere.
Private transfer: nullifier = receipt position (pid); the receiver marks
          it claimed in a sparse Merkle tree over the 40-bit position
          space, verified in-circuit (SMT.VerifyInsert: 2 x 40 node
          hashes); the tree root lives inside the account commitment.
          Receipt-tree opening has the same depth (40) and is bound
          to the witnessed position.
R_op:     one circuit for both operations; a witness bit selects the
          branch, so sends and receives are indistinguishable on the
          wire and cost the same to prove.

End-to-end private transfer [pedersen] (receipts d40, nullifier SMT d40): Alice sends 300 to Bob
  send + receive verified, ledger stores one commitment per account,
  tampering, wrong-position, receive-replay, and double-claim rejected

End-to-end private transfer [poseidon] (receipts d40, nullifier SMT d40): Alice sends 300 to Bob
  send + receive verified, ledger stores one commitment per account,
  tampering, wrong-position, receive-replay, and double-claim rejected
